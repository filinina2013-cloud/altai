Three trips that took place and two that did not. Interview stories are paraphrased from research notes. AI-generated portraits are illustrative and do not depict the actual participants.

P01

Travelled · summer Altai

Went to summer Altai with organisers already known from a Baikal trip.

Before booking, asked who would lead the group and whether evenings were free. Afterwards, would have liked transfer times, group size and personal time stated upfront.

What mattered

Transfers, group size, personal time

P03

Did not travel · places ran out

Considered a calm two-day trip but did not book before the places ran out.

Checked whether the programme was mandatory, how logistics worked and how free socialising would be. Not a drop-out caused by the interface.

What mattered

Mandatory programme, logistics, freedom to socialise
