## Rule: colour compliance (all sections, all iterations)

Every colour comes from ALTAI / Colors in Figma. Dark surfaces use the Dark-mode values. There are no new or approximated shades, and nothing is sampled from references. The system has no opacity tokens, so a reduced opacity is applied to an approved colour and the value is stated. Each delivery lists the applied tokens and their HEX values outside the frame.
